# Brand PR shortlist (draft for Allaia's approval)

**Phase 2:** this starts after the Phuket and Phang Nga villa outreach finishes (about 25 Nov). The categories are activewear, clothing, dresses, and luxury skincare, make-up and perfume.

**The rule:** a brand must ship to Bali (Indonesia). MARIEMUR could not ship to Bali, so every brand's shipping is checked on its own site before it gets a pitch.

**How sure I am that each brand ships to Indonesia:**
- **ID**: the brand is Indonesian or Bali-based, so it ships here for certain.
- **SEA**: a Singapore or Southeast Asia brand that usually ships to Indonesia.
- **INTL**: a global brand known to ship internationally. Indonesia still needs checking.

These ratings come from what I already know. Each one is verified on the brand's shipping page before it is pitched.

## Before any brand is pitched
1. **Check shipping.** Confirm on the brand's own shipping page or checkout that it delivers to Indonesia. If it doesn't, mark it `no_ship`.
2. **Find the right inbox.** Look for a PR, influencer or creator email, or an official creator or ambassador application form. Prefer the APAC or Indonesia team where one exists.
3. **Use a brand pitch,** not the resort template. It will offer product-led content (Reel, carousel, Stories) with optional paid usage, and use proof from brand collabs: WISKII, Mealira, Aemeralds and the Jason Derulo campaign.
