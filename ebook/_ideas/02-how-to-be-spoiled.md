## The angle that fits the brand

Same thesis as Paid to Exist: know your worth, then charge it. This time it's love.
Men provide for the woman they're already proud to be seen with. She's soft, sure of her standards, and has a full life that doesn't wait for him.
The book is about becoming her, not about tricks. That keeps it in the voice of Become Her and protects the brand.

## Draft outline (to shape together)

1. Why some women are always spoiled and others always split the bill
2. The provider man: who he is, where he is, how to tell him from the man who only performs it
3. Your standards are the filter: what you accept on date one sets the next year
4. Soft, not available: feminine energy, receiving, letting him lead
5. Rooms where you meet him: travel, hotels, dinners, the life you already built
6. The first three dates: what you say, what you never pay for, what you never explain
7. Receiving gracefully: how to accept gifts, and how to make him feel like a man for giving them
8. Keeping him: the mystery, the full life, the woman he can't predict
9. Red flags: love-bombing, control, money with strings attached
10. Your own money: why being spoiled is a choice, never a need
